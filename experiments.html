MathML

E = m c 2

MathML has technically been part of HTML for years, but you couldn't really rely on it. According to caniuse.com, Chrome added it back in version 24, removed it because it was buggy, and didn't bring it back until version 109 in 2023 (same for Edge). Since most people browse on Chrome or Edge, using MathML meant most visitors would just see broken equations, so developers stuck with images or tools like MathJax until every major browser caught up.
